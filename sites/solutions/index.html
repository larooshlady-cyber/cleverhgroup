Why It Matters

Mould doesn't wait for a complaint.

For Guests

Mould doesn't stay in one room. Left untreated in an A/C coil or a wall cavity, it spreads through the air your guests breathe, every night of their stay.

For Families

Children are more vulnerable to mould exposure than adults, with a higher risk of respiratory irritation and allergic reactions.

The Full Solution Range

Four solutions. One protection system.

The A/C coil treatment is the core of the program, but mould does not stay in the plant room. Four solutions cover the full path, the A/C coil, the condensate tray, the room, and the wall. Each one shown here with its actual product label.

01

Foam Enzyme Cleaner

1L · ~15-min treatment

Coconut-derived multi-enzyme foam for hot A/C coils, HVAC, cold rooms, refrigeration and ice machines, non-corrosive, biodegradable, no need to dismantle the unit.

Core A/C coil treatment
02

Remediation Pastille

Fitted at every service

20 pastilles per box. Secured in the condensate tray with the clip or tie wire provided, keeps overflow out of guestrooms.

Odour Eliminator
