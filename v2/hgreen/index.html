Why It Matters

Mould doesn't wait for a complaint.

For Guests

Mould doesn't stay in one room. Left untreated in an A/C coil or a wall cavity, it spreads through the air your guests breathe, every night of their stay.

For Families

Children are more vulnerable to mould exposure than adults, with a higher risk of respiratory irritation and allergic reactions.

For the Planet

H Green is 100% biodegradable and non-toxic. Real protection for guests and staff, without the environmental or safety trade-off.
